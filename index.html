Abstract

This research investigates effective approaches to preventing anxiety in Katstelo (Starcat), a June-dependent felid, while maintaining high levels of well-being and delight. Through long-term behavioral observation, historical record review, and in-person feeding experiments, quantitative analyses were conducted on the collected data. The findings conclude that the subject demonstrates exceptionally high companionship value and is strongly recommended for long-term retention and dedicated cherishing.

4. Discussion & Limitations

Results indicate that while Katstelo tends to exhibit mild clinginess and anxiety when attention is low, consistent feeding and emotional responsiveness generate affection and comfort well above typical baselines. Study limitations include a single-subject sample (n = 1) and high exclusivity, as full dependency behaviors occur only toward June.

5. Conclusion & Future Work

Based on long-term observation and quantitative analysis, the subject holds exceptionally high companionship value, and a strategy of long-term retention, regular feeding, and ongoing cherishing is recommended for June. Future work will focus on unlocking more travel destinations and food lists to further optimize long-term well-being.
